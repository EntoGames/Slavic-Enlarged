# Slavic Enlarged

> A comprehensive Slavic overhaul for **Crusader Kings III** — 35 cultures, 4 faith branches, 15 holy sites and a reformation reward system.

![CK3 1.19](https://img.shields.io/badge/CK3-1.19-blue) ![Version 1.0.4](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-1.0.4-green) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-proprietary-lightgrey)

## What does this mod do?

Slavic Enlarged replaces the base game's broad Slavic cultures with historical tribes and gives Slavic paganism a three-branch regional structure with its own reformation mechanic.

### Features

- **31 new cultures** across Eastern, Western, Southern, and Polabian Slavic groups
- **4 corrections** to vanilla cultures (Croatian, Serbian, Pommeranian, Carantanian)
- **4 faith branches** — Eastern, Western, Southern, and Ancient (the common root)
- **15 holy sites** with unique piety and prestige bonuses
- **Reformation mechanic** — rewards scale with how many holy sites you control
- **Full localization** in English and Polish, with LLM translations for German, French, and Spanish

### Cultures

| Group | Count | Examples |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Eastern | 11 | Krivichians, Drevlyans, Polyans, Severian, Ilmenian |
| Southern | 7 | Travunians, Docleani, Timocani, Pannonian Slavs |
| Western | 5 | Polans, Goplans, Mazovians, Kashubians, Nitrianians |
| Polabian | 7 | Obodrites, Lutici, Wagrians, Rani, Sorbian |
| Vanilla corrections | 4 | Vistulan, Silesian, Pommeranian, Carantanian |

### Holy Sites

15 locations across three regions — Kyiv and Arkona are the strongest (+8% piety, +5% prestige each). Control 9+ to become a Great Reformer.

## Installation

1. Subscribe on [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3740630117)
2. Enable the mod in the CK3 launcher
3. Start a new game (recommended: 867 AD bookmark)

**No DLC required** for the base mod.

### Load order

```
1. Slavic Enlarged          (base — required)
2. Slavic Struggle of Perun (addon — optional)
3. Myths & Legends          (addon — optional)
4. Kingdom mod(s)           (optional)
```

## Compatibility

The mod is **92% additive** — all new content uses the `se_` prefix and doesn't touch vanilla files. The only overrides are 4 vanilla culture corrections (tradition/ethos adjustments for historical accuracy).

Safe with any mod that doesn't redefine: `croatian`, `serbian`, `pommeranian`, or `carantanian` cultures.
